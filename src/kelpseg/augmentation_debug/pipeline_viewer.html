          uint16 by dividing by 65535. Stock clipping operates in 0–1 units;
          output returns to raw units before the final 5000 cap and fixed
          normalization. The 0–2000 color display is separate. D4 moves all
          grids together. Original invalid image fill is restored after
          photometric operations; spatial dropout ignores targets under its
          holes. Background examples are diagnostic controls. This viewer shows
          the approved recipe; no training run is performed.
