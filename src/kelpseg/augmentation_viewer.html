          Display: raw 0–2000 (visual only). Model input cap remains 5000.
          Native uint16 imagery is scaled to float32 by dividing by 65535,
          without a 5000 pre-cap. Stock pixel transforms apply their native 0–1
          clipping, including the raw-zero lower bound. Values are converted
          back to raw units before the final model cap and fixed normalization.
          Original invalid image fill is restored after photometric transforms;
          stock spatial filters still see zero nodata/padding near boundaries.
          Labels: yellow kelp, purple ignored or unavailable support. Background
          controls are diagnostics only. These wide isolated sweeps are
          exploratory; the approved recipe is reviewed in the separate combined
          pipeline viewer.
